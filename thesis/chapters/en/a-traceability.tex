sounds absent from Russian remain distinct.

\emph{sukun} closes a syllable.

\chapter{Preregistration summary}\label{app:prereg}

The preregistration (version 0.5, to be approved and frozen before data collection) fixes:
\begin{itemize}
  \item the main hypothesis and three questions (RQ-3 additional); confirmatory hypotheses for RQ-1
    (Table~\ref{tab:hyp}), hypotheses for RQ-2 and RQ-3 with the supervisor; mandatory descriptive reports (the
    agentic loop's log, the best configuration's learning curve, the contribution of data sources, learners'
    learning curves) and exploratory analyses declared in
    advance (E-1, E-4--E-9); everything else is also exploratory;
  \item the unit of analysis (attempt) and clusters (learner);
  \item exclusions: stub attempts, unusable and off-task recordings (counted separately), attempts before the
    freeze; self-corrections in a separate block;
  \item participants: adults consenting to voice recording, model training and teacher tests; first language and
    reading experience from the entry questionnaire;
  \item annotation: error type (reading / pronunciation / unclear) by both teachers on test attempts and every
    tenth training attempt; a teacher test (system's estimates not shown) at entry, at the end of the pilot and delayed;
  \item the split for RQ-2: about 30\,\% of learners in the test set, the list frozen by fingerprint before the
    agentic loop first runs; test annotation by two teachers; configuration search (cross-validation, a log
    of every configuration, a fixed budget) and mastery-model parameters only on the training part; the test set
    opened once;
  \item the fallback: fewer than \textasciitilde1{,}500 annotated attempts by 15.04.2027 --- fine-tuning is
    removed from the agentic loop's components;
  \item the stopping rule: the planned end of the pilot; interim reports descriptive only;
  \item analysis: cluster-bootstrap intervals, mixed models, Holm's correction at $\alpha=0.05$;
  \item deferred: the earlier hypotheses H2.1--H4.2 were moved to the PhD plan (ADR-0064);
  \item freezing: the SHA-256 fingerprint and date are recorded in the research log; any change is a new
    version with a reason.
\end{itemize}
