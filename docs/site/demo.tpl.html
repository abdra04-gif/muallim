Muallim: learning to read Arabic from zero, inside Telegram

A reading trainer for Russian-speaking adults, built on al-Muallim ath-Thani. Every lesson follows one simple path: watch a short teacher video, hear the lesson words from a real teacher, read them aloud in a voice message, and get friendly feedback.

Inside a lesson

Four steps in one message

A lesson is a single interactive message that moves through four steps as the learner works. The video and the recordings arrive as ordinary Telegram messages around it, so they play with Telegram's own player.

How reading is checked

From a voice message to friendly feedback

The check runs on our server. It compares what the learner actually pronounced with what the lesson words require, sound by sound.

🎤Voice messageRecorded with Telegram's standard microphone
🎚Audio preparationConverted to a clean mono recording for the model
🧠Phoneme recognitionThe Quran Muaalem model hears which sounds were read
⚖️ComparisonMatched against the expected sounds of each lesson word
💬FeedbackWords marked ✓ or !, at most 1–3 tips in plain language
🗂Research recordThe attempt is stored under a pseudonym for the study

What the check can notice

Wrong letter: one consonant read as another
Wrong vowel mark: fatha, kasra or damma mixed up
Missing vowel: a short vowel left out
Too short: a long vowel not held
Too long: a short vowel stretched
Missing shadda: a doubled consonant not doubled
Missing sound: a sound skipped
Extra sound: a sound that is not in the word
Feedback encourages and never blocks. The learner always sees what went well first, gets only the most important tips, and can move on. Perfecting the articulation points is a long process that a real teacher completes over time.

Principles

What the design commits to

Arabic script and sound onlyNo transliteration in Cyrillic letters. A sound is taught by hearing it, not by spelling it.
Real teachers, real voicesAll videos and recordings are made by people. No synthetic voices or generated images.
Strict book orderLessons follow al-Muallim ath-Thani. Lesson words use only the letters already taught.
A light pathThe minimum is to read every lesson's words once. Feedback is given, perfection is not demanded.
Short lessonsOne to three letters per lesson, a video of at most five minutes.
EncouragementProgress inside the lesson and overall, congratulations, and a streak that motivates without punishing.
Built on Telegram's interactive messages

What is interactive and what is a regular message

Since November 2026 a Telegram message can contain a small interactive app. Muallim uses it for navigation and progress, and keeps media as regular messages.

Interactive messagesRegular Telegram messages
