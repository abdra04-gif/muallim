Today · The task

Hear the mistakes, don’t guess the words

A learner reads a text we already know: a letter with a vowel sign, a syllable, a word. We need to know which sounds were actually said, including the wrong ones. In research this is called mispronunciation detection, not ordinary speech recognition.

Ordinary recogniser

“Which word was most likely said?”

It is built to fix what it hears and write a real word. A mistake disappears on the way.

Whisper on the syllable بَ answered بي, نعم, لا — and wrote a vowel sign in only 3 of 840 clips.

Sound-level checker

“Which sounds were actually produced?”

It writes sounds, not words, and has no language model to “correct” them. A wrong sound stays visible.

Same kind of clip → b a. Now the bot can compare it with the book and explain the error.

Muallim · speech checking research

2

Key point: ordinary speech recognition is the wrong tool. It is trained to output the most probable word, so it hides exactly what we need to see. That is why the bot uses a model that writes sounds (phonemes) without a language model.

Today · How it works

What happens to one voice message

1

Voice message

The learner reads a line of the book and sends a voice note in Telegram.

2

Clean-up

ffmpeg turns it into standard audio: 16 kHz, one channel.

3

Listening

The Muaalem model writes the sounds it heard and how each was made.

4

Comparing

Sound by sound against the book. Rules and thresholds decide what counts as an error.

5

Explaining

The learner gets the result and each error explained in Russian.

Kept every time

The audio, the text from the book, the model’s raw answer, the model and rule versions. Teachers add their labels later. This is already the seed of the dataset.

Research · The key idea

Check the expected letter, don’t guess

In the first lessons the answer is one of only 84 options (28 letters × 3 vowels). So we built four checkers on top of the strongest listening model and tested them on all 8,904 public clips — always on students the checker had never heard.

55.3%

88.0%

95.8%

Letter and vowel both right. +41 points from the change of design alone. Still weak: ظ and the pair ث / س. Must be retrained on our own learners.

Muallim · speech checking research

10

The main finding of the hands-on work. Free transcription asks the model "what did you hear?" Verification asks "is this the letter we expected, or one of its neighbours?" On beginner material the second question is much easier. When the checker is allowed to say "not sure" on a quarter of clips, it is right 96 percent of the time on the rest.

The pipeline keeps everything that already works and adds two steps: a quality check before the model and a verifier after it. Сегодня · Задача

Слышать ошибки, а не угадывать слова

Ученик читает текст, который мы знаем заранее: букву с огласовкой, слог, слово. Нужно понять, какие звуки он на самом деле произнёс, в том числе неправильные. В науке это называется обнаружением ошибок произношения, а не обычным распознаванием речи.

Обычный распознаватель

«Какое слово, скорее всего, сказали?»

Он создан исправлять услышанное и писать настоящее слово. Ошибка по дороге исчезает.

Whisper на слог بَ ответил بي, نعم, لا — и написал огласовку лишь в 3 записях из 840.

Проверка по звукам

«Какие звуки на самом деле прозвучали?»

Пишет звуки, а не слова, и у неё нет языковой модели, которая бы их «исправила». Неверный звук остаётся видимым.

Такая же запись → b a. Теперь бот может сравнить с книгой и объяснить ошибку.

Muallim · исследование проверки чтения

18

Главное: обычное распознавание речи — не тот инструмент. Оно обучено выдавать самое вероятное слово и поэтому прячет как раз то, что нам нужно увидеть. Поэтому бот использует модель, которая пишет звуки (фонемы) без языковой модели.
